## 3. ACCEPTANCE & TESTING PROCEDURE / ПОРЯДОК ПРИЕМКИ И ТЕСТИРОВАНИЯ

3.1. **Delivery for Review.** Upon completion of each Milestone, the Developer will deploy the Deliverables to a staging/test environment and submit written notice to the Client together with an **Acceptance Certificate (Schedule B)**.

3.2. **Review Period.** The Client shall have **[7 (seven)] business days** (the "**Review Period**") from the delivery date to test the Deliverables against the agreed specifications in Schedule A.

3.3. **Approval or Defect Notice.** Within the Review Period, the Client shall either:
- (a) Sign and return the Acceptance Certificate; or
- (b) Provide a consolidated written list of reproducible non-conformities ("**Defect Notice**") detailing deviations from the agreed specifications in Schedule A.

3.4. **Defect Rectification.** If a Defect Notice is timely provided, Developer will correct verified reproducible defects within a commercially reasonable timeframe (typically **[5 to 10] business days**) without additional charge, after which the Deliverable will be re-submitted.

3.5. **Deemed Acceptance (Автоматическая приемка).** If the Client:
- Fails to submit either a signed Acceptance Certificate or a detailed Defect Notice within the Review Period; or
- Puts the Deliverables into live commercial / production use or makes them available to end-users,  
the Deliverables for that Milestone shall be conclusively deemed accepted in full without reservation ("**Deemed Acceptance**"), and the corresponding payment obligation shall become immediately due and payable.  
*(Если Заказчик не предоставил мотивированный список дефектов в течение срока проверки либо запустил проект в боевую эксплуатацию, этап считается принятым в полном объеме).*
